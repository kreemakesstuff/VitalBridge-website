The Reality

Decades of health history, trapped in plastic bags.

When patients walk in with unorganized reports, specialists have barely five minutes—forcing them to guess past trends or order redundant tests.

45+ Page Chaos

Patients accumulate deteriorating physical prescriptions and unsearchable lab PDFs. In emergencies, vital history is entirely lost.

3-Minute Consults

Doctors lack the operational bandwidth to dig through document dumps, leading to diagnostic blind spots and adverse interactions.

3-Day Delays

Hospitals and TPAs drown in paperwork. Manual bill verification halts cashless admissions and discharge approvals for days.

The Ecosystem

From consumer wedge to enterprise infrastructure.

MedStory (B2C App)

Will be a free, zero-friction mobile health vault for chronic patients to digitize legacy records.

— Will provide instant physical prescription & lab report scanning
— Will generate 30-second synthesized clinical summaries
— Will establish seamless linkage via Ayushman Bharat Accounts

VitalBridge (B2B SaaS)

Will deliver turnkey digital infrastructure for independent clinics, hospitals, and health insurers.

— Will offer a ₹999/mo ABDM-compliant terminal for clinics
— Will create zero-setup hospital gateways for discharge summaries
— Will feature 2-Minute Insurance APIs for cashless approvals

Deep-Tech Moat

Deterministic extractive AI.

Standard LLMs hallucinate. Our architecture will be purpose-built for the complexities of high-stakes Indian healthcare data.

Domain-Specific OCR

Will be trained to handle noisy Indian prescriptions, hybrid handwritten-printed slips, skewed mobile scans, and diverse lab layouts.

Zero-Hallucination

Will extract, verify, and link exact text coordinates directly back to the original source PDF for flawless physician auditing.

Cryptographic FHIR

Unstructured data will be transformed into standardized FHIR-compliant bundles tied to ABHA IDs for national interoperability.

Execution

A focused two-year roadmap.

Phase I: B2C Adoption

We will launch MedStory, our free mobile health vault for chronic patients & digital caregivers.

The platform will extract active medications and lab histories in under 30 seconds. We will enable frictionless Ayushman Bharat registration while stress-testing our OCR accuracy on over 10,000 real-world Indian medical records.

Phase II: B2B Enterprise

We will deploy plug-and-play ABDM-compliant infrastructure across the network.

We will roll out clinic terminals and zero-setup hospital gateways for verified discharge summaries. We will scale per-call API adjudication queries allowing payers to instantly verify records for cashless approvals.
